How to convert Jira wiki markup to Markdown

  1. Copy from Jira. Open the ticket and copy its description or a comment — formatted text and raw wiki markup both work.
  2. Paste into the left pane. Rich formatting and Jira wiki markup (h1., *bold*, {code}, ||header|| tables) are auto-detected and converted instantly.
  3. Copy clean Markdown from the right pane — GitHub Flavored Markdown that pastes cleanly into GitHub issues, GitLab, Notion, Obsidian, or any README.

This free online Jira to Markdown converter runs entirely in your browser — nothing you paste ever leaves your machine.
